The date is on your calendar

Should I text my ex happy birthday?

You can care about the date without sending a message. Before texting, ask whether you would still send it if you knew the reply would be a single “thanks”—or no reply at all. If the birthday is mainly giving you a socially acceptable reason to reopen contact, keep the message unsent. A calendar notification does not create an obligation.

Last reviewed: August 31, 2026

What to do in the next 10 minutes

  1. Write the birthday message exactly as you want to send it.
  2. Delete every line that asks a question, refers to the relationship, or hints at meeting.
  3. Picture three outcomes: a warm reply, “thanks,” and silence.
  4. Notice which outcome your message is secretly built to produce.
  5. Wait until the timer ends. You are allowed to choose no message.

The birthday text is not always just a birthday text

You have been in strict no contact

A birthday exception can reset a boundary you built for a reason. Not sending does not make you cruel; it may simply keep the boundary consistent.

You ended on friendly terms

A brief greeting may be uncomplicated if contact is already calm and neither person is using it to test the relationship. Keep it genuinely brief.

You hope they will remember your birthday later

That turns the greeting into an unspoken trade. If the future return message matters, the text is carrying more weight than “happy birthday.”

You bought a gift before the breakup

Owning the gift does not require delivering it. Decide about the object separately from deciding whether to reopen contact.

When contact may actually be necessary

A birthday itself is not usually a practical necessity. Contact may still be needed for shared-child plans, a scheduled handoff, work, property, money, or an urgent safety matter that happens to fall on the same date. Keep that message about the necessary issue rather than combining it with emotional birthday contact.

What not to decide from the date

  • That this is your only respectful option.
  • That a warm birthday reply proves they want more contact.
  • That silence makes your message embarrassing or wrong.
  • That remembering the date means you should act on it.
