Before you press send

Should I text my ex?

Usually, not from the first rush. Put the message somewhere private for ten minutes before you decide. Ask what you want the text to do, whether your ex can realistically give you that, and how you will feel if there is no reply. If it is not about safety, shared responsibilities, money, property, or another real deadline, it can wait.

Last reviewed: August 31, 2026

What to do in the next 10 minutes

  1. Copy the exact message into a private draft. Do not improve it yet.
  2. Name the hoped-for result in one line: reassurance, reunion, apology, an answer, or relief.
  3. Write the response you fear most, including silence.
  4. Wait until the timer ends before reopening their chat.

The point is not to prove that texting is always wrong. It is to make sure the first surge is not making the decision for you.

The moment matters more than the wording

You want to send “I miss you”

Missing someone is real, but the text often asks them to remove the feeling for you. A reply may calm you briefly without changing why the relationship ended.

You found an old photo or song

A memory can make the past feel current. Give it ten minutes before treating nostalgia as new information about the relationship.

You drafted a “final” closure message

If you have written several final messages, this one may be another attempt to keep the conversation open. Save the unsent version and read it tomorrow.

You want to show that you have changed

A text can announce change, but it cannot demonstrate it. Ask whether sending now serves them, serves you, or mainly asks for immediate recognition.

When contact may actually be necessary

Contact can be practical when there is a shared child, home, bill, item, work task, legal document, health emergency, or clear safety issue. Keep the message limited to the necessary topic: one fact, one request, and one next step. If there is danger or a threat, use appropriate local emergency or professional support rather than relying on this tool.

What not to decide from the first urge

  • That silence means they never cared.
  • That one warm reply means the relationship is repaired.
  • That discomfort means you made the wrong decision.
  • That tonight is your last chance to say the perfect thing.
